PLAY INFORMATION:

The year is 1945 and it's total war. The Red Army and the "Wehrmacht" are fighting
for final victory. For some unknown reason this final battle takes place somewhere
out in the desert and to win this senseless war, you've to capture as much flags as
possible out of the enemy flagroom, deep in their base. Bringing these flags to the 
capturepoint of your own base will make your leader happy. That's all, Private.
Now get your rifle and kick some ass!
