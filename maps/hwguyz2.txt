Hwguyz  beta 2
by Axl Axl Axl Go Stl Rams!
www.AxlsTFC.com

Grab the enemies flag. Bring it back to your 
capture point. First team to cap 10 times wins.
You get 1 life per round. If you die, you get sent
to the spectator room. Rounds last 2 minutes. 
If no team caps after 2 minutes, round ends in a 
tie. Each team can have 2 medics.
